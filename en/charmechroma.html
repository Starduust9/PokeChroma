    The Shiny Charm

    What is the Shiny Charm?


    The Shiny Charm is an item introduced in Pokémon Black 2 and White 2. You get it after completing the National Pokédex, and it greatly raises your odds of meeting a shiny Pokémon. It is available in every main series game released since then.

    You go from 1/8192 to 3/8192 (about 1/2730) chances of getting a shiny Pokémon in Gen 5, then from 1/4096 to 3/4096 (about 1/1365) from Gen 6 to the latest game.

    Careful: once you have it, the Shiny Charm can't be turned off. So think it through before getting it.

    How to get the Shiny Charm in each game + odds with and without it


    Note that Mythical Pokémon are not needed to get it.

    Complete the National Pokédex
    Then talk to Prof. Juniper in Nuvema Town    		 1/8192
    ✨ 3/8192 (about 1/2730)
    Complete the National Pokédex
    Then talk to Prof. Sycamore in Lumiose City    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Complete the National Pokédex
    Then talk to Prof. Birch in Littleroot Town    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Complete the regional Pokédex (same for USUM)
    Then, in the GAME FREAK building, a developer gives it to you    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Complete the regional Pokédex
    Then, in the Celadon Condominiums, the GAME FREAK director gives it to you    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Complete the regional Pokédex
    Then, in the Hotel Ionia, the GAME FREAK director gives it to you    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Complete the National Pokédex
    Then, in a house by Lake Valor, the game director gives it to you    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Research level 10 on all 238 Pokédex pages
    Then, in Cyllene's office at Galaxy Team HQ    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Complete the regional Pokédex
    Then talk to Jacq in the biology classroom    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
    Research level 50 in Mable's research
    Then talk to Mable in the Pokémon Lab    		 1/4096
    ✨ 3/4096 (about 1/1365)
