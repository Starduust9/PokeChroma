Getting started with shiny hunting

Everything you need to know before your very first hunt ✨

What is a shiny Pokémon?

A shiny Pokémon has different colours from the usual ones and a small icon in its summary. When it appears in battle, it comes with a special animation and sound. The animation changes from game to game.

What is shiny hunting?

Shiny hunting means looking for, and catching, shiny Pokémon. There are several ways to find them depending on the game.

Shinies have existed since Gen 2 (Gold, Silver and Crystal). You can also get some in Gen 1, but their colours will only show once the Pokémon is transferred to a newer game (see the Gen 1 page).

You'll find every method, game by game, in Games.

Your odds of finding a shiny

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 8192 up to Gen 5 (Black, White, Black 2, White 2). Since Gen 6 (X and Y), it is 1 in 4096.

Doing lots of encounters does not raise your odds for the next one. Picture a 8192-sided die with a single shiny face: every encounter rolls the die again, and it may land on the right face… or not.

Meeting a shiny Pokémon is random (except with RNG, see below). Any Pokémon can be shiny as soon as there is an encounter: hatching an egg, a starter, tall grass, surfing… unless it is shiny locked.

Trainers' Pokémon are never shiny, unless the developers made it so. For example, in Gen 3 the Ralts that Wally catches during the tutorial can be shiny (1 in 8192), because it is a real encounter and it isn't locked.

Your odds can be raised with shiny hunting methods or with the Shiny Charm, an item introduced in Black 2 and White 2.

RNG

(Random Number Generator)

A computer can't create true randomness: the game simulates it with RNG. By predicting it, you can get a guaranteed shiny. It is a long and complex technique that consists of calculating the right moment (the right “frame”) to get a specific Pokémon. It is also widely used in speedrunning.

RNG works differently in each game. I don't explain it here, but I recommend the videos of Hari, who made many tutorials, or MaitreArmand's Écailles Rouges (in French): older and longer, but fascinating, as the topic is covered in depth.

Full odds shiny hunting

Hunting full odds means looking for shinies only at the base rate: 1 in 8192 up to Gen 5, then 1 in 4096 since Gen 6. Full odds hunters use neither methods that raise the odds nor the Shiny Charm.

As MaitreArmand (a major full odds hunter since 2007) writes on his Twitch:

“I only truly care about shinies with a probability of 1/8192 or less of appearing. Since XY, where the base probability of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1/4096, I have raised my tolerance up to that limit.”

Whether you hunt full odds or not is your own choice. Pokémon is a game: as long as your hunts make you happy, play them the way you like ✨
