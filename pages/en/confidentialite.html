Privacy policy

What the PokéChroma website and the PokéChroma Counter app do with your data. Last updated: November 2026.

In short

  • No selling of data. The website shows Google ads to fund the project: you choose whether they can be personalised.
  • No ads in the PokéChroma Counter software.
  • An account is optional: the site and the counter work without one.
  • You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time.

Signing in with Discord or Google

If you sign in, we only receive from Discord or Google: your account ID, your display name, your profile picture and your e-mail address. With Discord, we also receive your server tag and avatar decoration if they are visible. We never receive your password.

This information is only used to create your PokéChroma profile and to find your data on all your devices. Your e-mail address is never shown publicly.

What is stored

  • Visible to everyone: your nickname, bio, avatar, badges, shinies (Pokémon, game, encounters, method, Ball, nickname, location, date) and the 3D detectors you choose to share.
  • Visible only to you: the state of your hunts, your friends list and your friend requests.
  • Anonymous: the number of counters open (“online”) is computed with a random number, not linked to your identity.

Data is hosted by Supabase (database) and the website by Cloudflare.

Contact form

Messages sent from the Contact page (nickname, e-mail, message) are forwarded to me by e-mail through the Web3Forms service, only so I can reply.

Advertising

To help pay for the site, Google AdSense ads may be shown on pokechroma.com and in the web app. Google and its partners may use cookies to show these ads and measure how well they work.

On your first visit, a window asks for your consent. You can refuse personalised ads: you will then see non-personalised ads. You can change your mind at any time with the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of the page.

Learn more: how Google uses advertising cookies.

The PokéChroma Counter software shows no ads.

On your device

The website only keeps your language, your online counters and, if you sign in, your session (to keep you signed in) in your browser's storage. Your language and counters never leave your device. The PokéChroma Counter app keeps your hunts and settings in its own folder, on your PC.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all your online data, or for any question, write to me through the Contact page.
