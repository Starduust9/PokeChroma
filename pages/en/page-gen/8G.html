Getting a shiny in Gen 8

What to know

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 4096, and 1 in 1365 with the Shiny Charm, in all three games.

The hunting methods of this generation:

  • Sword and Shield: the number of Pokémon defeated, Dynamax Adventures and the Masuda method.
  • Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl: the Poké Radar and the Masuda method.
  • Pokémon Legends: Arceus: mass outbreaks and the Pokédex research level.

Sword and Shield

Number of Pokémon defeated

The Pokédex counts how many times you've defeated or caught each species. The higher that number, the better your odds of meeting that Pokémon as a shiny. From 500, your odds reach about 1 in 683, and about 1 in 512 with the Shiny Charm.

Pokémon visible in the grass respawn when you walk away: chain battles against the same Pokémon, then hunt it once you've reached 500.

Dynamax Adventures (The Crown Tundra expansion)

Each Pokémon met during a Dynamax Adventure has about 1 in 300 odds of being shiny, and about 1 in 100 with the Shiny Charm. The legendaries at the end of an adventure count too: it's the best way to get them shiny.

The Masuda method

The odds rise to 1 in 683, and to 1 in 512 with the Shiny Charm. See my Masuda page.

Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl

The Poké Radar

The Poké Radar is back, as in Diamond and Pearl: you get it after the National Pokédex. Build a chain of encounters with the same Pokémon in the shaking grass; from a chain of 40, shiny patches show up much more often.

The rules to avoid breaking the chain are the same as in Gen 4: see the Gen 4 page.

The Masuda method

The odds rise to 1 in 683, and to 1 in 512 with the Shiny Charm.

Pokémon Legends: Arceus

No battle needed here: shinies are visible right in the overworld, and a sound plays when one appears near you.

Research level

Reaching research level 10 for a Pokémon slightly raises your odds, and completing its Pokédex page (“perfect”) raises them even more. Combine it with the Shiny Charm to benefit everywhere.

Mass outbreaks

During a mass outbreak, dozens of Pokémon of the same species appear: it's the best method in the game. With the Shiny Charm and a perfect Pokédex page, your odds reach about 1 in 128 per Pokémon.

Massive mass outbreaks (added by the game's free update) are a bit less generous, but they feature several species at once and can be refreshed by travelling between areas.
