Getting a shiny in Gen 4

The beginning of hunting methods

A shiny still appears with a unique sound, but the stars are now blue. There are always 7 or 9 stars swirling when the shiny appears.

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 8192.

Specific to Diamond, Pearl and Platinum: the star in the shiny Pokémon's summary is red, just like its Pokédex number.

The hunting methods of this generation:

  • The Poké Radar: a post-game item to chain Pokémon; the odds for the chained Pokémon can reach 1 in 200.
  • The Masuda method: breeding 2 Pokémon from different countries raises the odds to 1 in 1638. (Named after Junichi Masuda, who revealed the method — his Wikipedia page.)

The Poké Radar

You get the Poké Radar from Prof. Rowan in Sandgem Town once you have the National Pokédex.

It doesn't work in very tall grass, mud, water, caves or the Great Marsh. Using a Max Repel is highly recommended to avoid running into a Pokémon outside the radar.

When you use it, up to 4 grass patches shake around you, each hiding a Pokémon from the route. After you knock it out or catch it (catching raises the odds of good patches on the next use), up to 4 patches may shake with the same Pokémon, which continues the chain. Reach a chain of 40 identical Pokémon to get 1 in 200 odds of a shiny.

Once you reach 1 in 200, just keep using the Poké Radar until you see the shiny patch.

Recharging the Poké Radar

To use the Poké Radar again, you need to walk 50 steps each time. Recharging doesn't break the chain, and it gives you new shaking patches! You can recharge it as many times as you want to find the “perfect” patch and keep your chain alive.

Finding the perfect patch

When you use the Poké Radar, patches shake in a 9×9 square around your character.

Always stand in the middle of the grass (as in the picture) so that the “special patches” land in grass, otherwise they are lost.

Some patches barely move, some move a little and some move a lot. Pick one that moves a lot.

If you're unsure or didn't see the patches clearly, don't take the risk: step out of the grass without going too far, walk 50 steps to recharge the radar and use it again. The chain won't break! Better to recharge than to risk breaking the chain.

Once the chain reaches 40, the odds are 1 in 200 (the maximum). From then on, just keep recharging the Poké Radar without starting an encounter (you can leave the grass to recharge it) until you see a shiny patch, which guarantees a shiny Pokémon.

What breaks your chain:
  • Running from an encounter.
  • Riding the bike.
  • Turning the console off or closing the game.
  • Going too far from the grass area.
  • Stepping on a patch that barely moves.
  • Not stepping on the patch right above your character (its head hides it, so you may miss that it barely moved).
