Getting a shiny in Gen 1

How can you get a shiny when they were only created in Gen 2?

What makes a Pokémon shiny already existed in Gen 1: its DVs (Determinant Values). To be shiny, a Pokémon needs DVs (Gen 1 and 2) or IVs (Gen 3 onwards) with specific values.

So you'll need an unusual hunting method: check the Pokémon's stats and compare them with the ones given by a tool, to know whether the Pokémon will be shiny once transferred.

TheHolomovement made an excellent tutorial that explains everything about Gen 1 shiny hunting in less than 15 minutes.

Here are the Pokémon that can, or cannot, be shiny:

✅ Can be shiny

  • Static Pokémon
  • Gift Pokémon
  • Fished Pokémon
  • Glitch Pokémon (e.g. Mew)

❌ Cannot be shiny

  • Tall grass Pokémon
  • Cave Pokémon
  • Surfing Pokémon
  • Safari Zone Pokémon

What to know before a hunt

  • You'll need several Rare Candies to level your Pokémon up and check its stats.
  • After the transfer, your Pokémon will always be in a Poké Ball: I recommend duplicating Master Balls to make catching easier.
  • The Pokémon's nature is set by its experience points.
  • If the Pokémon has its hidden ability, it keeps it; otherwise it gets its default ability.
  • IVs are random, except 3 that are always 31, and all EVs are reset to zero.

(There are very easy item duplication glitches here.)

A tool to check whether the Pokémon is shiny

First Generation Shiny Hunting Tool by @Magikarpii

Some Pokémon are harder to check than others: some only need to level up, while others must be checked several times depending on their level. The tool automates everything as long as you enter the right information.

First, pick the Pokémon you're hunting at the top left, then below it the level it will have when you get it (encounter, trade, etc.). If the Pokémon evolves during the checks, tick “Faire évoluer?” — this is optional, you can also cancel the evolution and leave it unticked.

Then press “Calc”: the stats the Pokémon must have to possibly be shiny after the transfer are displayed. Follow the check steps if there are any. If the stats don't match, press “<<Back”. If the Pokémon is shiny after checking, a red message is displayed.

You can find the tool on @Magikarpii's X or on MediaFire.
