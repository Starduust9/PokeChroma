Getting a shiny in Gen 7

What to know

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 4096, and 1 in 1365 with the Shiny Charm.

The hunting methods of this generation:

  • SOS chaining: Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon.
  • Ultra Wormholes: Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon.
  • The Masuda method: Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon.
  • Catch combos: Let's Go Pikachu and Let's Go Eevee.

SOS chaining (Sun, Moon, Ultra Sun, Ultra Moon)

During a battle, a weakened wild Pokémon can call an ally for help. Every ally that shows up grows the chain, and your shiny odds grow with it. After 30 calls, they reach about 1 in 315, and about 1 in 273 with the Shiny Charm.

Bring a Pokémon that knows False Swipe to weaken the Pokémon without knocking it out: a weakened Pokémon calls for help more often. Also bring items to restore PP (Ethers, Elixirs), as a chain can last a long time.
The chain breaks if the calling Pokémon faints or is caught before you've found your shiny. Keep it alive and knock out the allies that show up.

Ultra Wormholes (Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon)

Travelling through Ultra Wormholes with Solgaleo or Lunala, you can meet legendaries and Ultra Beasts. The further you go (in light-years) and the bigger the wormhole's ring, the higher the shiny odds: from 3000 light-years, they can exceed 1 in 3.

It's the best way to get shiny legendaries in Gen 7.

The Masuda method

Hatching eggs from two Pokémon of different nationalities raises the odds to 1 in 683, and to 1 in 512 with the Shiny Charm. Everything is explained on my Masuda page.

Catch combos (Let's Go Pikachu and Eevee)

In Let's Go, Pokémon are visible in the grass. Catch the same Pokémon several times in a row without stopping: that's a catch combo. From 31 catches in a row, your shiny odds jump: about 1 in 273 with the Shiny Charm and a Lure.

The combo breaks if you catch a different Pokémon or if the Pokémon flees. Running from a battle doesn't break it.

Bonus: in Let's Go, shinies are visible right in the grass, before the battle even starts.
