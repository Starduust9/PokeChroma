Getting a shiny in Gen 9

What to know

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 4096, and 1 in 1365 with the Shiny Charm.

As in Legends: Arceus, Pokémon are visible in the overworld: you can spot a shiny right away, without starting a battle.

Scarlet and Violet

Mass outbreaks

A mass outbreak makes lots of Pokémon of the same species appear. The more you defeat or catch during the outbreak, the better your odds: a first step at 30, then the maximum from 60.

Sparkling Power (sandwiches)

Some sandwiches give Sparkling Power for one Pokémon type. At level 3, it greatly raises your odds for that type for 30 minutes. You need special ingredients (Herba Mystica) to get it.

The most effective combo: a mass outbreak with 60 Pokémon defeated + a level 3 Sparkling Power sandwich + the Shiny Charm. Your odds then reach about 1 in 512 for every Pokémon of the outbreak.

The Masuda method

The odds rise to 1 in 683, and to 1 in 512 with the Shiny Charm. See my Masuda page.

Pokémon Legends: Z-A

In Legends: Z-A, Pokémon live in Lumiose City's wild zones. A simple technique is to reset a wild zone: look at the Pokémon there, then leave the zone (or fast travel) and come back. The zone's Pokémon are generated again, with a new chance of being shiny each time.

The 3D mode of PokéChroma Counter automatically counts the Pokémon you meet in a wild zone, cycle after cycle. I use it for my Fletchling hunts ✨
