Getting a shiny in Gen 6

Halved odds and a boom in hunting methods

A shiny still appears with a unique sound and stars. In the Pokémon's summary, the star is red.

Big change: the chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon becomes 1 in 4096, twice as likely as before. With the Shiny Charm, it rises to 1 in 1365.

The hunting methods of this generation:

  • The Masuda method: hatching eggs from two Pokémon from different countries.
  • Chain fishing (X, Y, Omega Ruby, Alpha Sapphire).
  • The Friend Safari (X and Y).
  • The Poké Radar (X and Y).
  • Hordes: 5 Pokémon at once, so 5 chances per encounter.
  • The DexNav (Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire).

The Masuda method

Hatching eggs from two Pokémon of different nationalities raises the odds to 1 in 683, and to 1 in 512 with the Shiny Charm.

Since X and Y, a Pokémon's nationality depends on the language chosen at the start of the game, no longer on the cartridge. Everything is explained on my Masuda page.

Chain fishing

Keep fishing in the same spot: every time a Pokémon bites (“Oh! A bite!”) and you battle it, the chain goes up, and your odds with it. After about twenty catches in a row, they reach roughly 1 in 100.

The chain breaks if nothing bites (“Not even a nibble…”) or if you move. Use a Repel and fish from a spot with no tall grass around you.

The Friend Safari (X and Y)

After the League, the Friend Safari in Kiloude City offers areas linked to your console's friend codes. The Pokémon found there have better shiny odds: about 1 in 819.

It's one of the best methods of the generation, since you can chain encounters very quickly.

The Poké Radar (X and Y)

It works as in Gen 4: build a chain of encounters with the same Pokémon in the shaking grass. The longer the chain, the better the odds, with the maximum from a chain of 40.

Every tip to avoid breaking your chain is on the Gen 4 page.

Hordes

With the move Sweet Scent (or Honey), you trigger a horde of 5 Pokémon. Each one has its own chance to be shiny, so you get 5 times more chances per encounter: about 1 in 819.

The DexNav (Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire)

The DexNav lets you spot a hidden Pokémon and sneak up on it. The more you chain encounters with the same Pokémon and the higher your search level for that species, the better your shiny odds.
