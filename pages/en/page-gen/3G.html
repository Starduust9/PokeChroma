Getting a shiny in Gen 3

What to know

A shiny always appears with yellow stars and a unique sound.

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 8192. There is no method to raise it.

Specific to Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald: there is no star in the shiny Pokémon's summary, but its Pokédex number is golden.

Soft resetting

Resetting is a technique to find a specific shiny Pokémon: you keep restarting the game in front of a specific Pokémon until it is shiny.

It works on starters, legendaries, trades and static Pokémon. In FireRed and LeafGreen, the roaming Raikou, Entei or Suicune is generated after you give the Sapphire to Celio in the One Island Pokémon Center.

The roaming legendary depends on your starter:
Raikou for Squirtle
Entei for Bulbasaur
Suicune for Charmander

Latias and Latios, who roam in Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, are generated after you become Champion: when you watch TV in Ruby and Sapphire, or when your mom brings it up in Emerald.

  • Latias appears in Sapphire and Emerald if you answer “Red”.
  • Latios appears in Ruby and Emerald if you answer “Blue”.

The soft reset lets you restart the game quickly without turning the console off, with the button combination A + B + Start + Select.
