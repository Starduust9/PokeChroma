Getting a shiny in Gen 2

The beginnings of shiny hunting

The first shiny arrives in Gen 2 with the famous Red Gyarados of the Lake of Rage, which introduces players to shinies, since you must meet it during the story.

The special sound that plays when a shiny appears also dates from this moment, and it has stayed throughout the whole series.

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 8192. There is no method to raise it.

There is, however, a trick to get a shiny with a 1 in 64 chance: in this generation, being shiny depends on IVs. Thanks to IV inheritance, if the parent of the opposite gender to the baby (or a Ditto) is shiny, the egg has a 1 in 64 chance of inheriting the shiny trait. (Only in Gold, Silver and Crystal.)

Breeding basics in Gen 2

The Day Care

The Pokémon Day Care is on Route 34, just south of Goldenrod City.

Getting an egg

Good to know: the Pokémon that hatches is always the same species as its mother. Two Pokémon of different species can produce an egg together, as long as they belong to the same egg group.

Only Ditto can breed with any egg group, and the baby will be the species of the other Pokémon.

Soft resetting

Resetting is a technique to find a specific shiny Pokémon: you keep restarting the game in front of a specific Pokémon until it is shiny.

It works on starters, legendaries, trades and static Pokémon. Watch out: the Raikou, Entei and Suicune trio, who roam all over Johto, are generated at the first wild encounter and not at the Burned Tower as in HGSS.

The soft reset lets you restart the game quickly without turning the console off, with the button combination A + B + Start + Select.
