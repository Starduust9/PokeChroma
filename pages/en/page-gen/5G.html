Getting a shiny in Gen 5

The arrival of the Shiny Charm

A shiny still appears with a unique sound and blue stars. There are always 6 stars swirling when the shiny appears.

The star in the shiny Pokémon's summary is red, just like its Pokédex number.

The chance of meeting a shiny Pokémon is 1 in 8192, but the Shiny Charm arrives in Black 2 and White 2, and stays in every game after that. It raises the odds to 1 in 2730.

Find my page about the Shiny Charm in every game here.

The hunting methods of this generation:

  • Lucky auras: only in Black 2 and White 2, in the +++ or S versions. They slightly raise the odds of finding a shiny while they last.
  • The Masuda method: breeding 2 Pokémon from different countries raises the odds to 1 in 1365. (Named after Junichi Masuda, who revealed the method — his Wikipedia page.)

Lucky auras (Black 2 and White 2)

First, unlock them by levelling up your Entralink.

  • For the +++ aura: reach level 49 ★Black and ☆White.
  • For the S aura: reach level 100 ★Black and ☆White.

S auras are as effective as +++ auras; only their duration and cost differ. So prefer the S aura when you can.

The base odds become 1 in 4096. With the Shiny Charm, they go up to 4 in 8192, about 1 in 2048.

This boost applies to every Pokémon, except eggs and shiny-locked Pokémon.
