The Masuda method

What is the Masuda method?


The name “Masuda” comes from Junichi Masuda, who revealed the method. His Wikipedia page here.
It has existed since Diamond and Pearl and is still around in the latest games.

To use this method, simply breed 2 Pokémon from the same egg group but of different nationalities! For example, a French Pokémon and an English Pokémon.

Before Gen 6, a Pokémon's nationality depends on the cartridge's language. From X and Y, it depends on the language you pick at the start of the game, so you can have a French cartridge with Korean Pokémon.
