PokéChroma Counter

The free shiny counter that counts your encounters by itself and alerts you when the Pokémon is shiny ✨

For Windows 10 and 11 · free · about 60 MB · updates install by themselves from the app.

What it does

🎮 Counts by itself

It reads the picture from your capture card (Elgato, OBS…) and adds +1 for every encounter or reset, without you touching anything.

✨ Shiny alert

A sound, a message and a Discord notification as soon as a shiny appears, so you never miss one again.

🌍 3D games

Legends: Z-A, Arceus, Scarlet / Violet: it spots every Pokémon on screen. You can train your own detector and share it with the community.

🗂 Several hunts

Run several hunts at once, with +1 / −1 keyboard shortcuts and a mini counter that stays on top of the game.

📺 For streamers

A page to add in OBS to show your counter live.

🏆 My shinies

Your shiny collection with badges, statistics, import from Pokémon Elements, an online profile and friends (sign in with Discord or Google).

Installation

  1. Download the Compteur-Shiny.zip file with the button above.
  2. Right-click it → Extract All…, then open the folder.
  3. Run CompteurShiny.exe. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info then Run anyway (the app isn't signed, which is normal for a fan project).
  4. Click New hunt, choose your Pokémon and your game, and off you go ✨

Install the PokéChroma app

Your hunts, your profile, your friends and every guide of the site in one app, on your phone's home screen or your PC's Start menu. It also works offline.

In Google Chrome, tap the “📲 Install the app” button, or the three dots at the top right, then “Add to Home screen” and “Install”.

In Safari (or Chrome), tap the Share icon , then “Add to Home Screen” and “Add”.

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon on the right of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow), or the “📲 Install the app” button.

For automatic counting with your capture card, use PokéChroma Counter for Windows instead.
