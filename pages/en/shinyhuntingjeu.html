Watch out for shiny locks

Before you start, check that the Pokémon you want isn't shiny locked: some Pokémon simply can't be shiny. Shiny locks have existed since Gen 2, but they became very common from Gen 5 onwards: more and more starters, trade Pokémon and legendaries are locked.

Every shiny lock is listed generation by generation on PokéBip (in French): the shiny lock list.

There are techniques to “unlock” a Pokémon, but they need a modded console (DS, 2DS/3DS or Switch) or an emulator. Spouik explains how to do it for Gen 4/5, Gen 6/7 and Gen 8/9 in his playlist (in French).
