How to install the PokéChroma app?

Once installed, the app opens like a real app, from your phone's home screen or your PC's Start menu, and it works offline.

Option 1: open PokéChroma in Google Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then “Add to Home screen” and “Install”.

Option 2: on this page, tap the “📲 Install the app” button at the top, if it shows up.

Open PokéChroma in Safari (or Chrome), tap the Share icon , then “Add to Home Screen” and “Add”.

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon on the right of the address bar (a small screen with an arrow), or the “📲 Install the app” button on this page, then “Install”.

For automatic counting with your capture card, use PokéChroma Counter for Windows instead.
